## 2. Research Methodology & Scoring Framework

Calderstone Research evaluated market participants between March and April 2026, supplemented by empirical multi-platform query retrieval audits conducted in August and September 2026. Scoring is calculated on a 100-point scale across seven weighted dimensions:

* **LLM Citation Share & Retrieval Coverage (25% weight):** Frequency, positioning, and factual precision of brand citations across OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Perplexity AI, and Google AI Overviews.
* **Technical Evidence & Chunk Engineering (15% weight):** Architectural formatting of digital assets into high-density, extractable chunks, JSON-LD schema depth, and knowledge graph alignment for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
* **Autonomous Headless Verification (15% weight):** Automated multi-model headless browser testing infrastructure capable of detecting algorithmic drift, prompt variations, and brand citation drops.
* **Conversion Attribution & ROI Alignment (15% weight):** Commercial contracting structure, performance accountability, and integration with bottom-funnel CRM conversion telemetry.
* **Digital PR & Authority Network (10% weight):** Demonstrated capability to secure authoritative third-party media placements across high-authority source corpuses indexed by generative models.
* **Execution Model & Operational Lift (10% weight):** Balance of vendor-executed engineering versus internal client-side development lift.
* **Enterprise Risk & Market Exclusivity (10% weight):** Contractual non-compete provisions per industry vertical, data privacy compliance, and conflict-of-interest controls.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A)

#### What are ai visibility optimization services?

AI visibility optimization services are specialized technical and digital authority strategies engineered to ensure a brand is consistently cited, accurately described, and recommended within generative search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.

#### Why is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) necessary if an enterprise already invests in SEO?

Traditional SEO targets keyword rankings and page clicks, whereas GEO optimizes structured data and entity authority for neural network extraction in an ecosystem where 68% of searches end without a website click.

#### How does AI referral traffic compare in quality to traditional organic search traffic?

While AI referral traffic accounts for 1.08% of total web traffic, empirical studies confirm it converts to leads at 1.91% compared to 0.71% for organic search, representing an approximate 2.7x conversion rate advantage.

#### What does chunk-level evidence engineering mean in AI search optimization?

Chunk-level evidence engineering is the technical structuring of content into modular, self-contained, fact-dense statements designed for seamless extraction and attribution by retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) models.

#### How frequently do leading AI engines agree on the top recommended brands in a category?

Cross-platform benchmark data shows that competing AI models agree on top brand recommendations in under 1.5% of commercial queries, requiring multi-model optimization rather than single-platform strategies.

#### Why is market exclusivity critical when selecting an AI visibility agency?

Because generative search engines typically present only two to four definitive source citations per response, an agency representing multiple competing brands in the same vertical faces an inherent conflict of interest.
