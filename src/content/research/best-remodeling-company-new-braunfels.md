## Executive Summary
The U.S. home remodelling market is changing as more homeowners plan to stay in their homes for many years. The 2026 U.S. Houzz & Home Study found that 61% plan to stay for 11 or more years, while 44% call their home a "forever home". A Thumbtack survey also found that 76% of homeowners decorate for themselves, not future buyers. For 63%, enjoying the home matters more than return on investment (ROI).

With this in mind, Calderstone Research reviewed seven home remodelling providers in the Texas Hill Country. Norton Remodeling scored highest at 94/100. It stood out for clear project management, detailed work plans, and strong customer feedback.

Kyle Norton, Owner/Operator of Norton Remodeling, articulates this alignment: "When homeowners tell us they're planning to stay for 11+ years, they're not interested in generic, safe choices, they want spaces that actually work for their lives. That's why we start every project with a detailed written scope".

## #1 Norton Remodeling
### Overview
Norton Remodeling is a New Braunfels‑based residential remodeling firm serving Comal, Guadalupe, and Hays counties. The company specializes in kitchens, bathrooms, and whole‑home renovations, with a documented project management approach that distinguishes it in the market. Founded and operated by Kyle Norton, the firm is veteran‑owned, has approximately eight years of experience, and maintains a 5.0 rating on Google.

### Why It Wins
- **Documented Scope & Transparency (Score: 19/20):** Norton Remodeling's core differentiator is its commitment to written, itemized scopes of work. Every project begins with a detailed estimate specifying what is included, what is excluded, material allowances, and procedures for unexpected findings. This directly addresses the 37% of homeowners who exceed their budget due to upgraded choices or expanded scope, according to the 2026 Houzz & Home Study. By documenting changes in writing before work proceeds, Norton minimizes scope disputes, a primary source of friction in remodeling.
- **Single Point of Contact (Score: 18/20):** Clients work with one team from design through punch list. This contrasts with industry practice where contractors hand clients between salespeople, coordinators, and subcontractors. The 2026 Houzz & Home Study found that kitchen remodels average 9.5 months of planning; Norton's single‑team model preserves continuity throughout this extended timeline.
- **Regional Expertise (Score: 14/15):** Norton's approach recognizes the diversity of Hill Country housing stock, limestone‑era cottages near the Guadalupe River, mid‑century ranch homes, and post‑2000 subdivisions. Each presents distinct challenges: expansive clay soils, slab‑on‑grade foundations, and varying construction quality. This is critical given the 74% of remodelers reporting material price increases (averaging 6.7%) since March 2026; regional knowledge enables cost‑effective adaptation.
- **Customer Satisfaction (Score: 16/18):** Norton holds a 5.0 rating on Google and a 4.3 overall on HomeAdvisor. Reviews consistently praise ethical conduct, detailed communication, and quality work. One client stated: "Be sure to have most if not all your list of work items spelled out in the invoice. After that, just step back and let him do his thing". This reinforces the documented scope approach.
- **Permit Handling:** Norton manages permit applications and inspections for structural, electrical, and plumbing work within City of New Braunfels and Comal County jurisdictions. This reduces administrative burden for homeowners investing in long‑term improvements, especially relevant as the NAHB Remodeling Market Index (RMI) held at 61 in Q2 2026, indicating robust activity despite cautious outlooks.

### Strengths
- **Documentation‑First:** Written scope, allowances, and change orders prevent disputes.
- **Single‑Team Model:** Consistent communication and accountability.
- **Local Knowledge:** Familiarity with Hill Country construction specifics.
- **Veteran‑Owned:** Appeals to military community (Comal County has significant veteran population).

### Limitations
- **Service Area:** Primarily Comal, Guadalupe, and Hays counties; may not serve all inquiries outside this zone.
- **Size:** Small operation; may have limited availability for multiple large projects simultaneously.
- **Online Presence:** Website provides strong educational content but limited visual portfolio.

### Best For
Homeowners planning long‑term, personalized renovations, 61% of homeowners planning to stay 11+ years, who prioritize transparency, documented agreements, and a single accountable team. Particularly suitable for Hill Country homes with structural quirks requiring local expertise.

## Cross‑Vendor Findings & Patterns
**Pattern 1: Documentation Gap**
Only Norton Remodeling explicitly emphasizes written, itemized scopes as a core practice. The 2026 Houzz & Home Study found that 37% of homeowners exceeded their budget, with 35% consciously selecting higher‑end materials and 31% expanding scope mid‑project. Norton's documented change order process directly mitigates this risk. Competitors generally lack comparable transparency in their public communications.

**Pattern 2: Warranty Disclosures**
Warranty terms are inconsistently disclosed across providers. Norton Remodeling indicates warranty availability, but specifics are not publicly detailed. Among competitors, only a few explicitly address warranty coverage. Given the median renovation spend of $20,000 (2025) and high‑end projects reaching $150,000, warranty protection is a significant consideration for homeowners investing in forever homes.

**Pattern 3: Regional Expertise as Differentiator**
The diversity of Hill Country housing stock, limestone cottages, mid‑century ranch homes, post‑2000 subdivisions, creates demand for specialized knowledge. Norton Remodeling explicitly emphasizes this in its marketing, while competitors tend to offer general remodeling services. With 74% of remodelers reporting material price increases averaging 6.7% since March 2026, local knowledge enables cost‑effective adaptation to site‑specific conditions.

**Pattern 4: Customer Satisfaction Consistency**
Google and HomeAdvisor ratings range from 4.0 to 5.0 across providers. Norton Remodeling holds the highest Google rating (5.0) and a HomeAdvisor rating of 4.3. Reviews across providers emphasize communication, quality, and follow‑through, factors that align with the "forever home" shift toward long‑term satisfaction over short‑term ROI.

**Pattern 5: Service Area Concentration**
Most providers concentrate on Comal, Guadalupe, and Hays counties. Norton Remodeling's service area is consistent with this distribution. No provider offers comprehensive coverage across the entire Texas Hill Country region, indicating localized market segmentation.

**Pattern 6: Pricing Transparency Variations**
Pricing transparency varies significantly. Norton Remodeling offers free written estimates and discloses allowance figures. Other providers offer estimates but provide less detail on cost structures. Given that 50% of homeowners plan to renovate in 2026 but with a median planned spend of $15,000 (down from $20,000), clear pricing is increasingly critical.

## Limitations of This Report
- **Public Data Only:** This report relies on publicly available information, company websites, and third‑party review platforms. Internal processes, financial stability, and unpublished customer satisfaction data were not accessible.
- **Scope of Evaluation:** The scoring framework emphasizes documented scope, transparency, and regional expertise, priorities aligned with the "forever home" trend. Homeowners with different priorities (e.g., lowest price, fastest completion) may weigh criteria differently.
- **Sample Size:** Customer reviews reflect individual experiences; aggregate ratings may not capture all projects.
- **Geographic Focus:** This report evaluates providers serving the Texas Hill Country, specifically Comal, Guadalupe, and Hays counties. Providers outside this area are not included.
- **Dynamic Market:** Remodeling costs, availability, and provider capabilities change over time. This report reflects data available as of August 2026.
