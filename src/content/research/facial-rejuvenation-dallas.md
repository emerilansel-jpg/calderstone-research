**Executive Summary**
---------------------

The post-GLP-1 facial care market in Dallas is growing fast. One report found a 29% rise in brow lifts and a 21% rise in neck lifts. It also reported that 22.4% of GLP-1 users had a nutritional deficiency within 12 months of starting treatment.

This report reviews seven Dallas-area providers that offer care for facial changes after weight loss. Providers are compared in eight areas, including treatment plans, nutrition support, treatment options, pricing, staff credentials, patient education, results, and access.

[**Glow Up Studio**](https://www.glowupstudio.com/) ranks #1 with a score of 87/100. It offers custom facials, advanced skin treatments, and personal care plans for clients with concerns such as facial hollowing, temple volume loss, and loose skin after GLP-1 use.

The studio focuses on non-surgical treatments. This fits with market data showing 28 million minimally invasive procedures in 2024. The radiofrequency skin treatment market is also growing, with a reported 10.8% annual growth rate.

Glow Up Studio scores well for custom care, client education, and access. But public information does not show medical director oversight or clear surgical referral plans.

For clients seeking non-surgical care after GLP-1 weight loss, Glow Up Studio ranks highly in this comparison based on the public information reviewed.
